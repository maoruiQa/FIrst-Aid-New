# First Aid New 1.3.3

## The Rush and the Reckoning

*Danger brings the rush. Survival brings the reckoning.*

Adrenaline now follows an encounter from the first visible threat through the fight and into its aftermath. Combat pressure can relieve pain and sharpen action, but a prolonged rush may leave Fatigue once the area is safe. A full-strength Adrenaline Injector can also bring a downed player back into the fight.

Applies to all nine maintained Forge 1.20.1, Fabric, and NeoForge 1.21.1, 26.1, 26.2, and 26.3 modules. The 1.21.11 legacy modules remain unchanged.

## Adrenaline and combat feedback

- Lowered the default adrenaline gain coefficient from `0.18` to `0.15`; administrators can still tune it with `/firstaid adrenaline gain <value>` (legacy `/firstaid suppression gain` still works).
- Changed adrenaline desaturation, visual obstruction, sound muffling, and tinnitus intensity to use linear response curves. Mild mode continues to reduce feedback strength, while dynamic remains the default mode.
- Reduced per-hit adrenaline accumulation while allowing valid player attacks against neutral and hostile creatures to build adrenaline and its pain relief.
- Kept the one-second adrenaline pain-relief grace period after adrenaline reaches zero.
- Pain relief now immediately clears First Aid injury debuffs and prevents them from being reapplied while relief is active; unrelated effects with the same name remain untouched when ownership cannot be confirmed.

## Adrenaline encounter and rescue update

- Stationary or embedded projectiles no longer trigger near-miss adrenaline or its client feedback; only projectiles that actually moved can do so.
- Valid player-versus-player attacks now add adrenaline, alongside attacks on hostile and neutral creatures.
- Visible hostile creatures can start an encounter; nearby creatures targeting the player or a recent nearby player attacker sustain a configurable minimum adrenaline intensity. Defaults: 16-block sight, 100-degree view, 12-block threat range, 30-second attacker memory, 0.20 minimum intensity. Existing custom attacker-memory settings are preserved.
- Medium adrenaline grants Haste I and high adrenaline also grants Strength I. Stronger or longer effects from other sources remain intact.
- After 60 continuous seconds of adrenaline, leaving all nearby threats triggers Fatigue once: Darkness I, Nausea I, Mining Fatigue I, and Weakness I for 10% of the adrenaline duration, capped at 30 seconds. The fatigue timer also counts Adrenaline Injector use; if adrenaline ends first, Fatigue waits until the area is safe.
- Administrators can configure Fatigue in the server config or with `/firstaid adrenaline fatigue on|off`, `threshold <seconds>`, and `ratio <percent>`; `/firstaid suppression fatigue` remains an alias. Disabling it prevents new episodes while active effects expire normally.
- The Adrenaline Injector now immediately fills adrenaline to maximum intensity and has four uses by default, including newly crafted injectors.
- An Adrenaline Injector can rescue a downed player, restoring collapsed critical parts to 1 health and applying the full injection effect. Its wake-up delay uses the defibrillator's shortening factor.
- Fixed the client rescue prompt and hold action to recognize the Adrenaline Injector in every maintained module, including Forge 1.20.1, and play its injection sound.
- Player-facing suppression labels now read Adrenaline. `/firstaid adrenaline` provides the existing command options; `/firstaid suppression` and existing configuration and save keys remain compatible.

## Builds

- Updated all nine maintained artifacts to `1.3.3+<loader><minecraft-version>`.
- Legacy modules are excluded from this release.
